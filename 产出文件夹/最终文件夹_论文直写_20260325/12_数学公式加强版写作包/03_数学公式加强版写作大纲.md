### 中文

面向车载低轨卫星通信系统的链路-干扰-端到端联合评估与最劣工况分析

### 英文

Joint Link, Interference, and End-to-End Evaluation with Worst-Case Scenario Analysis for Vehicle-Mounted LEO Satellite Communication Systems

## 摘要骨架

### 第一句：背景

低轨卫星车载接入系统具有显著的时变几何、频率复用和干扰耦合特征，因此需要统一的链路与 EMC 联合评估框架。

### 第二句：方法

本文建立了一个系统级数学模型，将星座构型、星地几何、CCI、jammer 注入、上下行链路选择、端到端时延与吞吐统一到同一时变框架中，并进一步引入 `InfoGAN + GA` 搜索最劣干扰场景。

### 第三句：结果

在当前工程配置下，本轮 MATLAB 完整运行得到：
- `DL Base = 78.97 Mbps`
- `DL Worst = 76.91 Mbps`
- `UL Base = 75.69 Mbps`
- `UL Worst = 52.19 Mbps`
- `E2E Base = 73.99 Mbps`
- `E2E Worst = 50.30 Mbps`

### 第四句：意义

结果表明，该框架能够同时表达卫星链路性能、EMC 干扰影响、端到端瓶颈和最劣工况退化，对车载 LEO 通信系统的方案设计与预评估具有工程价值。

## 2. System Model

## 2.1 Orbit and Constellation Model

先定义：
- 地球半径 \(R_E\)
- 引力常数 \(\mu\)
- 高度 \(h\)
- 轨道半长轴 \(a\)

正文公式建议：

\[
a = R_E + h
\]

\[
T_{\text{orbit}} = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{a^3}{\mu}}
\]

再定义：
- 轨道面数
- 每面卫星数
- 复用因子
- 相位因子

## 2.2 User/Gateway Visibility

正文公式建议：

\[
v_{U,i}(k)=\mathbb{1}\{e_{U,i}(k)>e_{\min}\}
\]

\[
v_{G,i}(k)=\mathbb{1}\{e_{G,i}(k)>e_{\min}\}
\]

\[
v_{r,i}(k)\approx \frac{r_{U,i}(k+1)-r_{U,i}(k)}{\Delta t}
\]

## 2.3 Reuse and ISL Routing Graph

正文公式建议：

\[
\chi_i = \big((p_i-1)+(s_i-1)\big)\bmod K_r + 1
\]

\[
\mathcal{G}=(\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E},\mathcal{W})
\]

\[
d_{\text{ISL}}(k)=\text{ShortestPath}\big(s(k),g(k)\big)
\]

## 3. Link and Interference Modeling

## 3.1 Free-Space Propagation and Received Power

正文公式建议：

\[
PL_i(k)=32.45+20\log_{10}(d_i(k)\text{[km]})+20\log_{10}(f\text{[MHz]})
\]

\[
P_{S,\text{DL},i}^{\text{dBm}}(k)=EIRP_{S,\text{DL}}^{\text{dBm}}-PL_i(k)+G_{r,\text{DL}}
\]

\[
P_{S,\text{UL},i}^{\text{dBm}}(k)=EIRP_{S,\text{UL}}^{\text{dBm}}-PL_i(k)+G_{r,\text{UL}}
\]

## 3.2 Co-Channel Interference

正文公式建议：

\[
P_{I,\text{self},i}^{\text{dBm}}(k)=EIRP_{I}^{\text{dBm}}-PL_i(k)+(G_r-\Delta_I)
\]

\[
P_{I,\text{tot},c}(k)=\sum_{i:\chi_i=c} P_{I,\text{self},i}(k)
\]

\[
P_I(k)=P_{I,\text{tot},\chi_{s(k)}}(k)-P_{I,\text{self},s(k)}(k)
\]

## 3.3 Jammer Coupling Model

正文公式建议：

\[
P_{J,m}^{\text{base,dBm}}(k)=EIRP_{J}^{\text{dBm}}-PL_{J,m}(k)+G_{r,J}
\]

\[
\theta_{m,i}(k)=\sqrt{\Delta az_{m,i}^2(k)+\Delta el_{m,i}^2(k)}
\]

\[
P_{J,\text{DL},i}(k)=\sum_{m\in\mathcal{J}} P_{J,m}^{\text{base}}(k)\, g_J(\theta_{m,i}(k))
\]

## 4. Dynamic Link Evaluation

## 4.1 Gateway and Serving Satellite Selection

正文公式建议：

\[
g(k)=\arg\max_{i:v_{G,i}(k)=1} e_{G,i}(k)
\]

\[
s(k)=
\begin{cases}
\arg\max_{i:v_{U,i}(k)=1} e_{U,i}(k), & \text{if AJ inactive}\\
\arg\max_{i:v_{U,i}(k)=1} \gamma_i(k), & \text{if AJ active}
\end{cases}
\]

## 4.2 Anti-Jam Dynamics

正文公式建议：

\[
\mathbb{1}_{J}(k)=\mathbb{1}\{P_J(k)>P_N \land jamAgg(k)>0.05\}
\]

\[
\widetilde P_J(k)=
\begin{cases}
P_J(k)\cdot 10^{-D_{\text{null}}/10}, & \text{if AJ active}\\
P_J(k), & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
\]

## 4.3 Core Performance Metrics

正文公式建议：

\[
\gamma(k)=\frac{P_S(k)}{P_I(k)+\widetilde P_J(k)+P_N}
\]

\[
BER(k)=\frac{1}{2}\operatorname{erfc}\left(\sqrt{\frac{\gamma(k)}{2}}\right)
\]

\[
\eta(k)=\min\left(\log_2(1+\gamma(k)),6\right)
\]

\[
THR(k)=\frac{B\cdot \eta(k)\cdot 0.8}{10^6}
\]

\[
BLER(k)=1-(1-BER(k))^{L_b}
\]

\[
f_D(k)=-\frac{v_r(k)}{c}f_c
\]

\[
\dot f_D(k)\approx \frac{|f_D(k)-f_D(k-1)|}{\Delta t}
\]

## 5. End-to-End Model

正文公式建议：

\[
THR_{E2E}(k)=\min\left(THR_{DL}(k),THR_{UL}(k)\right)
\]

\[
BLER_{E2E}(k)=\max\left(BLER_{DL}(k),BLER_{UL}(k)\right)
\]

\[
\tau_{E2E}(k)=\frac{r_{U,s(k)}(k)+r_{G,g(k)}(k)+d_{\text{ISL}}(k)}{c}
\]

## 6. Worst-Case Scenario Search

## 6.1 InfoGAN-Based Jammer Envelope

正文公式建议：

\[
y = G_\theta([z;c])
\]

\[
jamAgg(k)=\mathcal{I}(y),\quad k=1,\dots,K
\]

## 6.2 Optimization Objective

正文公式建议：

\[
f(x)= -\overline{THR}_{tar}
      + W_{\text{out}} \cdot outage
      + W_{\text{bler}} \cdot \overline{BLER}
      + W_{\text{energy}}\cdot \Pi(jamAgg)
\]

\[
\Pi(jamAgg)=\mathbb{E}[jamAgg^2]+10\max(0,duty-0.55)^2
\]

\[
x^\star = \arg\min_x f(x)
\]

## 7. Interference Identification as an Auxiliary Interpretation Module

## 7.1 Power-Aligned IQ Synthesis

\[
r(n)=s(n)+i(n)+j(n)+w(n)
\]

## 7.2 STFT Representation

\[
S(\tau,f)=\sum_n r(n)w(n-\tau)e^{-j2\pi fn}
\]

\[
P(\tau,f)=|S(\tau,f)|^2
\]

\[
I(\tau,f)=10\log_{10}(P(\tau,f)+\epsilon)
\]

## 7.3 Label Rule

建议把现有问题和改进版想法一起写清：

\[
JSR(k)=10\log_{10}\frac{P_J(k)}{P_S(k)}
\]

\[
ISR(k)=10\log_{10}\frac{P_I(k)}{P_S(k)}
\]

\[
INR(k)=10\log_{10}\frac{\max(P_I(k),P_J(k))}{P_N}
\]

\[
label(k)=
\begin{cases}
\text{none}, & INR(k)<\tau_N \ \text{or}\ (JSR(k)<\tau_J \land ISR(k)<\tau_I) \\
\text{tone/pbnj/mod}, & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
\]
